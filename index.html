What is TSA?

The Technology Student Association (TSA) is a national organization dedicated to students engaged in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM). We build leadership, teamwork, and technical skills through competitive events and community projects.

Featured Competitive Events

Webmaster

Design, build, and evaluate a chapter website adhering to annual design challenges.

Coding

Test your logic, syntax, and programming efficiency under pressure.

Digital Video Production

Produce a cinematic short film or commercial highlighting STEM innovation.
